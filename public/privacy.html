PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Cobun AI ("we", "us", or "our") collects when you visit cobunai.com. In short: there are no accounts, and we never ask for your name, email address or password.

Information We Collect

  • Usage data — Google Analytics records anonymous visit data, such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser
  • Nothing else — there is no sign-up, login, mailing list or comment system, so we hold no personal information about you

Stored on Your Device

The site remembers which page you were on (Today or Sources) in your browser's session storage. It never leaves your device and is cleared when you close the tab.

How the Brief Is Made

Headlines and summaries are written from public sources (blogs, RSS feeds, Hacker News, arXiv, GitHub and others) by an open-source AI model, Qwen 2.5, running on GitHub's servers. Nothing about your visit is sent to it.

Third-Party Services

  • Vercel — hosts the website
  • GitHub Pages — hosts the news data at data.cobunai.com
  • Google Analytics — anonymous usage analytics

Like any web host, Vercel and GitHub receive standard technical details, such as your IP address and browser, when your browser loads the site.

Cookies

Cobun AI itself sets no cookies. Google Analytics uses cookies to tell visits apart. You can block them in your browser settings or with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Links to Other Sites

Every story links to its original publisher. Their own privacy policies apply once you leave Cobun AI.

Children's Privacy

Cobun AI is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the date at the top.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: privacy@cobunai.com
